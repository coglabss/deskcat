DeskCat — bundled asset credits

The SOURCE CODE of DeskCat is MIT licensed, but these art/sound assets are
the work of their original creators and remain under THEIR licenses. Please
credit them and check their terms if you redistribute.

cat-art.zip
-----------

  Please support / credit the artist.
